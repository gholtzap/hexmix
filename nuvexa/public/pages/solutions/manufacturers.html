01
Order classification

Orders pulled out of the shared inbox

Orders, inquiries and complaints share one inbox. Nuvexa recognises the orders and routes each one to the responsible clerk — so nobody opens every email to find their work.

02
Customer-specific article mapping

Remembers how each customer orders

Old article numbers, abbreviations, a customer’s own product names: Nuvexa learns each customer’s wording and maps it to your current article. Template OCR never learned — Nuvexa does.

03
Added surcharge lines

Freight and packaging added, not forgotten

Clerks used to add freight, packaging and surcharge lines by hand on every order. Nuvexa adds them before the order is booked into SAP, so every order goes out complete and is confirmed the same day.

Real-world data

Your data doesn't need to be perfect.

Industrial master data was never built for AI. Nuvexa is designed for legacy article numbers, customer-specific descriptions, abbreviations, missing fields, unusual units and hundreds of thousands of SKUs.

Understands unstructured requests

Typos, abbreviations, free text, and inconsistent terminology are recognized for what customers actually mean.

Works with your existing master data

Nuvexa connects to your current catalog and systems without requiring a data-cleansing project first.

Learns your commercial logic

Preferred products, equivalents, and in-house matching rules become part of the workflow—not a spreadsheet next to it.

Your sales team stays the quality gate

Low-confidence results are presented for review; the release decision stays with the sales representative.
