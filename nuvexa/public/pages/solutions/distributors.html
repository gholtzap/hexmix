Nuvexa reads any tender, not just Updates. It matches every line item to your range, checks it against the spec and sends the result to your ERP and to every customer.

01
Spec and tender-text reader

Every tender, in any format

Updates file, PDF, scan or plain email: Nuvexa reads them all and turns even long tenders into structured line items with quantities and long texts. Your project team bids on every project, not only the ones it has time to read.

02
Updates & PDF output

One tender, in every customer’s format

A same tender often goes out to 6–10 customers. Nuvexa sends it to your ERP and exports it as Updates and PDF for each one, long texts included, so your team no longer reformats every file by hand.

Order entry for your order desk

Every emailed order, straight into your ERP

Nuvexa turns the orders already arriving by email into ERP orders and learns your range and your customers’ wording as it goes. So you automate the orders your web shop never gets, without asking installers to change how they order.

Learn more about Orders

01
Order classification

Orders and call-offs pulled out of the inbox

Orders, call-offs, inquiries and complaints share one inbox. Nuvexa recognizes the orders, including those forwarded from branches, and routes each one to the right person on your order desk.

02
Customer-specific article mapping

Remembers how each installer orders

Installers order in their own words, with old or manufacturer article numbers. Nuvexa learns each customer’s wording and maps it to your article master. A lookup happens once, not on every order.

03
ERP order entry

Orders booked straight into your ERP

Approved orders go into your ERP as real sales orders, with header, lines and quantities. Your order desk gets time back for customers, advice and follow-ups instead of typing.

Real-world data

Your data does not need to be perfect.

Industrial master data was never built for AI. Nuvexa is designed for historic article numbers, customer-specific descriptions, abbreviations, missing fields, unusual units, and hundreds of thousands of SKUs.

Understands unstructured requests

Typos, abbreviations, free text, and inconsistent terminology are recognized for what customers actually mean.

Works with your existing master data

Nuvexa connects to your current catalog and systems without requiring a data-cleansing project first.

Learns your commercial logic

Preferred products, equivalents, and in-house matching rules become part of the workflow—not a spreadsheet next to it.

Your sales team stays the quality gate

Low-confidence results are presented for review; the release decision stays with the sales representative.
