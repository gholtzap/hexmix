Millions of people spend their days inside ERP systems – invaluable systems of record, but rarely built for people to actually work in efficiently.

Every day, skilled professionals lose valuable time to copy-paste and endless input masks, instead of selling, building, moving the business forward. That costs more than time – it costs growth, for individual companies and for the entire economy.

Our north star

No human should ever have to work manually in an ERP system again – so experts can get back to what they were actually hired to do.

Our starting point: automating sales for technical suppliers – a problem that’s big, painful, and costs millions in lost revenue.

Our story

A mission born in childhood.

“My family has been active in the plumbing and electrical trades for over Together. As the youngest in a fourth-generation trades business, I grew up bending pipes on construction sites. I experienced first-hand how slow and manual quoting in our industry is.

As an engineer at Google, I used data and AI to solve tough problems. This is when I teamed up with my co-founders Stefan and Sean to set out on a deeply personal mission: bring cutting-edge AI from Silicon Valley to the backbone of our economy. For the companies that keep our world running. For hard-working people like my family.”
